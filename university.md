# University

This section contains university coursework, module projects, reports, videos, and links to repositories.

I am currently studying Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Hull, where I have developed a strong foundation in robotics, software development, artificial intelligence, electronics, data analysis, and connected systems.

Throughout my degree, I have worked on a wide range of practical and theoretical projects that have allowed me to apply classroom knowledge to real-world engineering challenges. These projects have included autonomous robotics, ROS2 development, machine learning and AI systems, electronics and microcontroller programming, IoT devices, simulation environments, and data-driven decision making.

One of the aspects I enjoy most about university is the opportunity to combine multiple disciplines within a single project. Robotics requires an understanding of software, hardware, networking, data analysis, and problem-solving, which has helped me develop both technical skills and a broader engineering mindset.

Alongside my studies, I have taken an active role in the university community. As President of the Hull Robotics Society, I help support fellow students, encourage collaborative learning, and promote hands-on engineering projects. This role has strengthened my leadership, communication, and project management skills while allowing me to contribute to a growing community of students interested in robotics and technology.

My university experience has not only developed my technical abilities but has also reinforced the importance of teamwork, adaptability, and continuous learning. Through coursework, society activities, and independent projects, I continue to build the skills and experience needed for a future career in robotics, artificial intelligence, and advanced technology systems.
